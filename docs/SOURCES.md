# Source registry and authority

| Source | Use | Authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TİAK | Daily public top 10, methodology, monthly/yearly channel data | Primary | Public daily data covers only the top 10 in three audience groups. Full measurement data is commercial. |
| Official broadcaster pages | Show status, air date, episode pages, official summaries and trailers | Primary | ATV, Kanal D, Show TV, NOW, TRT 1 and other active networks. |
| Official YouTube channels | Trailer/full-episode discovery and publication timestamps | Primary | Store metadata and links; do not mirror video. |
| Dizilah | Calendar, series directory, episode numbering, ratings cross-check, cancellation buzz | Secondary | Excellent discovery layer; major claims still require primary confirmation. |
| Production companies | Cast changes, start/end announcements, renewals | Primary | Prefer direct announcements. |

## Rating caveat

TİAK says its detailed measurement results are contract-protected commercial data. Its public website exposes daily top-10 programs plus monthly and annual tables. DiziMeter must not imply access to non-public TİAK data. Values outside the public tables need a separately licensed source or clear secondary-source attribution.

## Copyright boundary

DiziMeter stores factual metadata, short attributed excerpts when necessary, source hashes, and original Persian analysis. It does not republish full recaps, subtitles, images, or articles from Dizilah or broadcasters.
